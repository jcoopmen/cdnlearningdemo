What reviewers look for

  • Practical takeaways participants can apply in their own work
  • Real examples, evidence, or results from projects you have led
  • Relevance to learning professionals working in Canada
  • A session design that involves the audience, not only a lecture

How to submit

  1. Review the guidelines

    Check the session formats, audience, and review criteria before you begin.

  2. Prepare your proposal

    Draft a title, a short abstract, learning outcomes, and a brief speaker bio.

  3. Submit online

    Complete the online form by 11 December 2026. You will receive a confirmation email.
